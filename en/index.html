YOUR GLOBAL DIRECT DISTRIBUTION PARTNER

Chumpol Trading Group Company Limited.

CTG – efficiency gains from direct integration of procurement, logistics, and delivery.

In the globally interconnected mobile communications industry, specialised distributors play a key role in the value chain. Chumpol Trading Group Company Limited has been a reliable distribution partner and trade supplier for the European market for smartphones and electronics for many years.

In our role as a distributor, we connect international manufacturers of mobile devices, household and consumer electronics with professional trading partners, bundling procurement, supply and logistics into one central business relationship.

Chumpol Trading Group Company Limited has been operating as an internationally oriented B2B distributor in the global trading business since 2017. We focus on direct sourcing and reliable supply to the European trade.

Our structures are geared towards efficient processes, dependable product availability and predictable delivery schedules. Our goal is to provide economically robust trade supply with clear processes and reliable framework conditions.

Sourcing and Partner Network

Our network currently comprises 932 registered companies from the European trade and distribution environment.

Every trading company can use our services and support without restriction. Participation in the partner network is voluntary and not a mandatory requirement for successful cooperation.

A partnership offers companies that seek a long‑term and closer business relationship additional services and special benefits. The focus is on mutual reliability, high quality standards, short decision‑making paths and the joint development of growth potential. The partnership does not involve any additional fees or financial obligations.

Our goal is a strong, future‑oriented trading community in which partners and we complement each other, share opportunities and provide our customers with sustainable added value.

A partnership thus offers extended services and advantages in addition to the normal business relationship:

  • Flexible trade credit for growth Short‑term higher trade credit volume for large orders – fast, uncomplicated and without additional interest charges or equity participation considerations. Independently of existing rules, ongoing trade credit, payment terms and stock‑disposition agreements, interest‑free supplementary credits can be granted at short notice when required. The decision is market‑oriented and can be implemented within a few hours. This enables partners to realise larger projects immediately and expand their business.
    Your order. Your growth. Our support.
  • Professional EDI integration & logistics handling Partners gain access to our EDI system and thus to direct digital communication throughout the entire order and logistics process. From order transmission and order confirmation to delivery status, tracking and customs clearance support, relevant information is exchanged quickly, transparently and professionally. This reduces manual effort, accelerates processes and creates transparency across the entire goods flow.
  • Individual partner conditions Attractive terms tailored to turnover, product group and volume – including tiered, quantity‑based and individual project pricing.
  • Exclusive product access Earlier access to new products, pre‑launch information and exclusive SKUs. This allows partners to place new products ahead of the competition.
  • Priority allocation for scarce products Reserved quantities and preferred allocation during shortages ensure greater supply security – especially when products are in high demand.
  • Protected projects & qualified leads Deal registration with protected project pricing as well as the transfer of qualified leads and project inquiries. Together, larger customers and projects are developed in a targeted manner.
  • Confidentiality, transparency & access to protected information Through appropriate NDA arrangements, partners gain access – to the extent agreed – to confidential information and business areas relevant to close cooperation. This includes, for example, purchasing conditions, manufacturer agreements, product roadmaps, pre‑launch information, availability and allocation details, project and customer data, partner data, forecasts and strategic planning. At the same time, the confidential information of all parties involved is comprehensively protected from disclosure to third parties.
  • Exclusive partner support Prioritised support, personal contacts as well as pre‑sales engineering and configuration consulting support partners in complex projects and increase implementation speed.
  • Price & inventory protection Price protection in the event of price reductions on stock goods, as well as stock rotation and sell‑through support, reduce the partner's inventory and price risk.

Thanks to our deep industry knowledge, many years of experience and a worldwide supplier network, we guarantee you the highest quality, planning reliability and adherence to deadlines. Our service portfolio extends far beyond pure procurement:

  • Exclusive direct partnerships: We work exclusively and directly with the original manufacturers and source our smartphones as new goods directly from the production sites. As the link between production and trade, we supply exclusively to the trade and maintain long‑term distributor agreements with the manufacturers.
  • Market‑compliant goods: All products are designated by the manufacturer for the German/European market, CE‑certified, comply with EU specifications and are freely saleable.
  • Local warranty service: Our smartphones come with the usual manufacturer's warranty, which can be claimed locally in Europe in the event of a claim. Warranty processing is carried out in the respective country through authorised dealers.
  • Direct shipping & dropshipping: Direct from the factory without detours or third‑party involvement – for maximum margins and stable prices. On request, we handle fulfilment and shipping directly to your end customers, so you can reduce storage costs and expand your service offering.
  • Portfolio & brand management: Regular product updates and access to the latest market launches give you a competitive edge. We work closely with Apple, Samsung, Asus, Xiaomi and Huawei and offer you selected top models from these leading manufacturers.
  • Full‑service support: Individual advice, transparent processes and personal contacts accompany you from enquiry through to delivery.

With our flexible solutions, we adapt exactly to your requirements, whether you order large volumes or smaller quantities of our premium models. With CTG, you optimise your inventory, conserve your working capital and focus entirely on growing your business.

Rely on Chumpol Trading Group Company Limited: We connect manufacturers and trading partners worldwide, creating transparent, efficient and cost‑effective processes – from the production site to you or your customer.

As a full‑service distributor, we guarantee more than just hardware. We offer strategic partnerships with SAP support and process design through to market‑ready placement. Rely on our expertise for sustainable growth of your sales channels.

Through industry‑leading efficiency and innovative distribution solutions, we enable our partners to:

  • ► Legal certainty under German law (BGB/HGB) – a reliable foundation for international business
  • ► Minimise operational costs through automated supply chains
  • ► Achieve scalability without warehouse investments (via dropshipping)
  • ► Reduce risk through just‑in‑time logistics
  • ► Benefit from local warranty service in Europe
  • ► Gain competitive advantages through exclusive market access

With us, you get far more than just products. We are your reliable link between production and trade, and you benefit from a global network at home and abroad.

Direct Shipping

Our transport/distribution service is aimed at individual and wholesale traders and is carried out by direct shipping without detours via third‑party companies. Trade agreements and optimised transport routes are used to ensure price stability and margin optimisation.

Dropshipping

Our dropshipping solutions help you optimise delivery times, warehouse planning and management, rationalise stock planning and improve overall administrative efficiency. Dropshipping saves storage and administrative costs.

Portfolio & Warranty

Stay up to date through the manufacturer's distribution. As a partner, help shape the launch of new products at an early stage. All products come with full manufacturer warranty, which can be claimed locally in Europe in the event of a claim.

Incoterms

The Incoterms "DDP" (Delivered Duty Paid), including goods insurance equal to the value of the goods, ensure smooth transport of the goods without you having to worry about the import process.

Delivery

Enjoy reliable, fast and punctual deliveries throughout Europe, directly to your delivery address. Our reliable air freight service ensures efficient transport and guarantees on‑time arrival.

Business Relationship

Our partners are the driving force. Benefit from a stable business relationship that offers you a global network, a comprehensive product portfolio and the highest customer satisfaction in a win‑win relationship.
