ELECTRONIC DATA INTERCHANGE

EDI – Electronic Data Interchange

The EDI-Gateway (Electronic Data Interchange) is a central component of our digital infrastructure. It enables a standardised, automated and secure exchange of business data between partners in our network.

What is EDI?

EDI is the electronic exchange of structured business messages between different IT systems. Instead of manually creating and sending orders, invoices or delivery notes, these documents are automatically transmitted from your system to the system of your business partners.

This saves time, reduces errors and significantly speeds up the entire process.

Purpose and Benefits within the Partner Network

  • Automation: Reduction of manual processes and avoidance of transmission errors.
  • Speed: Faster processing of orders and deliveries.
  • Transparency: Real-time overview of the status of orders and deliveries.
  • Standardisation: Uniform data formats facilitate cooperation.
  • Efficiency: Relief of your internal resources for value-adding activities.

Legal Framework

All contracts and business relationships within our partner network are based on German commercial law (HGB/BGB). This provides a clear and reliable legal basis for all transactions.

The place of jurisdiction is the local court of the respective catchment area or registered office. This enables a fair and local clarification of legal issues.

Technical Integration

The EDI-Gateway is designed to integrate easily into existing ERP systems and other commercial software solutions. We support common data formats and interfaces to ensure smooth communication.

If you have any questions about technical integration, our support team will be happy to assist you.

Access for Partners

The use of the EDI-Gateway is available to all members of our partner network. Implementation is arranged in coordination with our technical support. Further information is available upon your inquiry about partnership.
