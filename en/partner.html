Partner Network

Chumpol Trading Group Company Limited.

CTG PARTNER NETWORK · RULES OF PROCEDURE · EXCERPT

Efficiency and growth

The CTG Partner Network connects legally and economically independent companies in long-term, reliable business relationships. The focus is on concrete economic benefits, short decision-making paths, mutual support and professional handling of confidential information.

Product Carousel – Making goods available quickly

If a partner requires a specific product at short notice, they can submit a request to the network via the partner network EDI hub. If another partner has suitable goods procured through CTG in stock, these can be made available for the required order at short notice. Likewise, partners can make suitable stock positions visible within the network – so that available goods can be deployed where they are currently needed.

Trade Credits – additional scope for partners with large orders

If a larger business opportunity arises at short notice, CTG can significantly expand the available trade credit at short notice, independently of existing agreements. This means that even extensive orders can be flexibly realised – in suitable cases, a decision can be made within a few hours.

A partnership is not a prerequisite for doing business with CTG. The partner network is an additional offering for companies that wish to expand their long-term business relationship with CTG and take advantage of the associated benefits.

02 · KEY SERVICES IN FOCUS

Payment Terms, Credits & Exclusive Benefits

The following services offer partners particular scope for action and competitive advantages.

Extended Payment Terms and Individual Credit Lines

Partner companies may – depending on the respective business relationship and individual economic assessment – benefit from extended payment terms or individually agreed trade credit lines.

The specific design is based, among other things, on turnover development, payment history, business potential, project structure and previous cooperation. Extended payment and credit arrangements can give partners additional scope for financing their ongoing business. A partner's initial payment term is 30 days net.

Flexible Trade Credits for Growth

In the case of exceptionally large orders or short-term project opportunities, the existing trade credit volume may be temporarily expanded. Independently of existing payment terms, ongoing trade credits or stock-disposition agreements, short-term additional trade credits may be granted without additional interest charges and without participation or ownership claims.

The decision is made taking into account the specific market and business situation as well as the previous cooperation and may, in suitable cases, be taken within a few hours. This enables partners to realise larger orders and projects at short notice without their growth being restricted solely by temporary liquidity limits.

The aim of this support is not to control or financially bind the partner, but to promote its independent growth. The partner remains independent in its entrepreneurial decisions and development.

Your order. Your growth. Our support.

Early Product Access & Exclusive SKUs

Partner companies may – to the extent available – gain early access to product novelties, product information and selected market information. This includes early briefings, pre-launch information and early access to relevant product and market information.

Depending on the product and manufacturer agreement, partners gain access to exclusive SKUs, retailer variants, special models or special product configurations. Such products open up additional opportunities for differentiation from competitors and support the development of their own customer offerings.

Deal Registration and Protected Projects

Partner companies may have qualified customer projects and business opportunities registered within the framework of an agreed deal registration. The aim is to protect the partner who has developed a project, identified a business opportunity or performed the specific customer work for a qualified project.

If the requirements are met, registered projects may be protected against unnecessary channel conflicts and supported with individual project prices, special conditions or project-related product planning.

Qualified Leads & Joint Project Development

If suitable business opportunities are available, qualified partners may gain access to relevant leads, customer inquiries and project inquiries. The transfer takes place in particular if a partner is particularly suitable due to its technical competence, regional access or industry experience.

Larger projects may be developed jointly if required – from offer planning and technical preparation to coordination of product availability and support in implementation.

Confidentiality & Protected Information Access

Need-to-Know Principle: You are not required to disclose internal business, customer, inventory or financial information to the network. Information is only exchanged to the extent necessary for a specifically agreed cooperation.

With an existing confidentiality agreement (NDA), partners gain access to selected confidential information – such as purchasing conditions, product roadmaps, pre-launch information, availability, forecasts, customer information, partner information, bonus and incentive systems, technical information or trade secrets.

Mutual confidentiality applies to both parties. Confidential information of the partner company is protected accordingly within the framework of the respective agreements. The obligation applies during the partnership and for three years beyond its termination, unless statutory or regulatory disclosure obligations exist.

Actual access to information is provided exclusively to the extent required and agreed. Confidential information may only be used for the purposes of the respective cooperation and is subject to the provisions of the respective NDA or other confidentiality agreement.

EDI & Digital Logistics Handling

Partner companies may gain access to digital communication and EDI systems. This enables a structured exchange of information along key processes of order and logistics handling.

Depending on the respective systems, the technical connection may include in particular electronic order transmission, order confirmations, delivery and status information, tracking and shipping data, availability information as well as automated data transfer and technical interfaces. Digitalisation reduces manual effort, minimises sources of error and creates greater transparency throughout the entire goods flow.

03 · ORGANISATIONAL FRAMEWORK

Rules of Procedure · Excerpt

The following provisions form the organisational framework of the partner network. The complete rules of procedure are available for download as a PDF.

1. Purpose and Self‑Understanding

The partner network serves the long-term development and strengthening of its partners. It creates a reliable framework for economic cooperation, joint market opportunities and the utilisation of existing resources. Existing customer relationships and registered business opportunities are protected.

2. Independence of Partners

Partner companies remain legally and economically independent. Each partner company acts in its own name and on its own account.

ReliabilityClear agreements and binding communication
QualityProfessional business processes
FairnessRespectful cooperation

GrowthEconomic development on a sound basis

3. Principles of Cooperation

Cooperation within the partner network is based on the following principles:

IndependenceEach partner company acts in its own name, on its own account and under its own responsibility.
No shareholdingThe partnership does not create any shareholding or joint management.
No automatic exclusivityMembership does not in principle create any exclusive supply commitment or obligation to cooperate with other partner companies.
No general membership feesNo general admission, membership, franchise, licence or system fees are charged for admission and membership.
No general minimum turnoverMembership does not in principle create any minimum purchase or minimum turnover obligation.
Separate agreementsSpecific trade, supply, credit, project, exclusivity or confidentiality agreements are concluded separately and bindingly.

4. Costs and Economic Basis

Participation in the partner network is free of charge. No admission fees, membership fees, franchise, licence or system fees are charged. CTG finances itself from regular commercial transactions.

5. Admission and Partner Status

The partner network is open to commercially active companies, in particular wholesalers, importers, distributors, specialist retailers, trading companies, online retailers and project companies. Admission is based on a written expression of interest or by invitation. The partner committee decides on admission.

6. Admission Procedure

Admission to the partner network is generally based on a written expression of interest or on invitation by Chumpol Trading Group.

There is no entitlement to admission. The decision is made after an individual review applying uniform principles.

6.1 Expression of Interest

The interested company first submits a written inquiry containing sufficient information about its corporate identity, business activities, commercial experience and the intended scope of a possible cooperation.

6.2 Preliminary Review

The preliminary review may assess in particular:

  • the identity and legal existence of the company,
  • the nature and duration of its business activities,
  • the reliability and professionalism of prior communication,
  • the economic plausibility of the intended cooperation,
  • the quality and traceability of business processes, and
  • the general suitability for long‑term cooperation.

6.3 Compliance Check

To the extent required for the specific business relationship, Chumpol Trading Group may request additional information or evidence. This may be necessary in particular to meet legal requirements, verify corporate identity or ensure proper business relationships.

6.4 Assessment

The assessment of an application is not based solely on individual turnover, size or location criteria. The decisive factor is an overall assessment of the company's suitability and the expected quality of a long‑term cooperation.

6.5 Decision

A partner committee and the management of Chumpol Trading Group decide on admission.

The decision on admission may:

  • be made immediately,
  • be made subject to further information or conditions, or
  • be taken after a reasonable period of business cooperation.

A rejection generally does not require a detailed justification, unless legal obligations provide otherwise.

7. Equal Treatment and Objective Assessment

Applications and existing partnerships are generally assessed on the basis of comprehensible business criteria.

Different treatment based solely on origin, nationality, company size, geographical location, religion or worldview is not part of the admission procedure.

However, different business terms may be permissible if they are based on objectively different circumstances, in particular:

  • the nature and scope of the specific business relationship,
  • payment and credit risks,
  • logistical requirements,
  • regional market conditions,
  • contractually agreed services, or
  • other objectively justified business criteria.

8. Rights of Partner Companies

Membership in the partner network confers, within the framework of the actual cooperation, the following fundamental rights in particular:

  • the right to professional and respectful treatment,
  • the right to be assessed according to the applicable principles of the partner network,
  • the opportunity to participate in elections and involvement processes in accordance with these rules of procedure,
  • the right to submit proposals and suggestions,
  • the right to appropriate information on material developments of the partner network, and
  • the protection of confidentially transmitted information within the framework of applicable agreements.

In addition, individual partner companies may be granted additional services or special conditions depending on the specific business relationship. However, this does not create any automatic entitlement of other partners to identical conditions.

9. Partner Committee

The partner committee supports the further development of the network, involves partners in fundamental issues and decides on the admission of new partners. The committee consists of 25 representatives of the partner companies. It has no power of representation for CTG; entrepreneurial responsibility remains with the management.

10. Confidentiality

Business and trade secrets as well as non-publicly accessible information about business activities, customers, suppliers, prices, conditions, strategies and internal processes are treated confidentially. The obligation applies during the partnership and for three years beyond its termination. Special confidentiality agreements (NDAs) take precedence over this provision.

11. Compliance and Integrity in Business Conduct

The partner network expects compliance with all applicable legal provisions for the respective business activity and recognised principles of proper business practice.

ComplianceThe legal and regulatory requirements applicable to the respective business activity are complied with.
Protection of confidential informationTrade secrets and confidential data are protected in accordance with the applicable agreements.
Fair dealingMisleading statements, inadmissible influence and actions that seriously impair trust in the business relationship are not compatible with the principles of the network.

12. Binding Business Principles

Each partner acts honestly, reliably and professionally, fulfils agreements made, maintains confidential information and complies with applicable legal provisions. Impermissible payments, bribery and unjustified granting of advantages are excluded.

13. Termination and Final Provisions

The partnership may be terminated by written declaration. CTG terminates a partnership for good cause, in particular in the event of serious breaches of contract, significant payment arrears, breach of confidentiality, fraudulent or unlawful conduct or significant compliance risks.

The partnership does not create any shareholding, joint management, power of representation or joint liability. Individual contracts and the General Terms and Conditions remain independently valid.
