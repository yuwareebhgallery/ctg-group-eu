OUR SERVICE OFFERING

SERVICES & CAPABILITIES

Our Service – Your strategic advantage in the global market

At Chumpol Trading Group (CTG), we combine state-of-the-art logistics solutions, exclusive product portfolios and holistic full-service concepts. We see ourselves not only as a supplier, but as a strategic partner for your sustainable growth.

Direct Factory Shipping DDP

Maximum security through our DDP Incoterms (Delivered Duty Paid). We assume full responsibility for the entire goods flow from the factory – including transport, customs formalities, taxes and seamless goods insurance right up to your delivery address.

Through our cooperation with global air freight leaders and final last-mile delivery by DHL, we guarantee planning reliability in a volatile industry.

Exclusive Brand Partnerships

Gain access to the premium brands of the mobile world: Apple, Samsung, Asus, Xiaomi and Huawei. Our global sourcing team ensures daily inventory updates.

You get access to the latest flagship models and limited editions – always brand new, in original packaging and fully CE-certified for the European market.

Our Strengths

Services that directly contribute to growth, supply security and efficient business processes.

Global Sourcing & Direct Market Access

Direct proximity to production sites and manufacturer contacts creates short decision-making paths, up-to-date market information and access to new products.

Flexible Logistics & Direct Shipping

From direct factory shipping to customised dropshipping and fulfilment solutions – we tailor the movement of goods to your business model.

Partnership for Growth

Individual terms, flexible trade credits, protected projects and prioritised support create additional scope for growth.

Flexible Trade Credits for Growth

Short-term higher trade credit volumes for large orders – fast, uncomplicated and without additional interest charges or equity participation considerations. Independent of existing rules, ongoing credits and payment terms, interest-free supplementary credits can be granted at short notice when required. The decision is market-oriented and can be implemented within a few hours.

Your order. Your growth. Our support.

Professional EDI Integration & Logistics Handling

Partners gain access to our EDI system and thus to direct digital communication throughout the entire order and logistics process. From order transmission and confirmation to delivery status, tracking and customs clearance support, relevant information is exchanged quickly, transparently and professionally – reducing manual effort and creating transparency.

Individual Partner Terms

Attractive terms tailored to turnover, product group and volume – including tiered, quantity-based and individual project pricing.

Exclusive Product Access

Earlier access to new products, pre-launch information and exclusive SKUs. This allows you to place new products ahead of the competition.

Prioritisation for Scarce Products

Reserved quantities and preferred allocation during shortages ensure greater supply security – especially when products are in high demand.

Protected Projects & Qualified Leads

Deal registration with protected project pricing as well as the transfer of qualified leads and project inquiries. Together, larger customers and projects are developed in a targeted manner.

Confidentiality, Transparency & Access to Protected Information

Through appropriate NDA arrangements, you gain access – to the extent agreed – to confidential information such as purchasing conditions, manufacturer agreements, product roadmaps, availability, allocations, project and customer data, forecasts and strategic planning. Your data and our information are comprehensively protected from disclosure to third parties.

Exclusive Partner Support

Prioritised support, personal contacts as well as pre-sales engineering and configuration consulting support you in complex projects and increase implementation speed.

Price & Inventory Protection

Price protection in the event of price reductions on stock goods, as well as stock rotation and sell-through support, reduce your inventory and price risk.

Strategic Partnerships at the Production Site

Our presence at key production sites enables short decision-making paths as well as a deep understanding of production processes, quality controls and supply chains. This positioning creates transparency, direct access to new product launches and the ability to respond quickly to market changes.

Personal Full-Service Support

Our support concept goes far beyond order processing. It includes:

  • Tracking with proactive notification of deviations
  • Individual price tiers based on volume and potential
  • After-sales support with dedicated contacts
  • Process optimisation of your entire supply chain

Custom Dropshipping & Fulfilment

  • Capital conservation: No major investment in your own warehouse staff or space.
  • Speed: Automated processes shorten the time from order to delivery.
  • No overstocks or write-offs – you react flexibly to demand and market changes.
  • Reliable planning security: your logistics change from a fixed cost structure to a variable, revenue-dependent service.
  • Competitive advantage: ideal for e-commerce platforms that want to offer a wide range of products "just-in-time".

Our Extended Services in Detail

  • Exclusive direct partnerships with manufacturers: As an authorised sales channel for established brand leaders, we guarantee continuous availability, market-compliant terms and exclusive access to new product launches.
  • Full market conformity and certification: Every product is brand-new original goods, complies with EU specifications and CE directives and is manufactured specifically for the German economic area.
  • Local warranty service and after-sales support: The devices are covered by the standard manufacturer's warranty, which can be claimed locally in the respective country if required.
  • Integrated direct shipping and dropshipping management: Automated interfaces to leading shipping service providers enable just-in-time delivery to your end customers – under your brand or neutrally.
  • Strategic portfolio and brand management: Our analytics team continuously monitors market trends, price movements and product life cycles in order to continuously strengthen your competitiveness.

Legal Certainty and Transparency

All contracts and business relationships with CTG are governed by German substantive law (BGB/HGB). This provides you, as a partner, with a clear, reliable and internationally recognised legal basis – for long-term, trustworthy cooperation.

Further information can be found in our General Terms and Conditions.

Individual Solutions for Your Business Model

We understand that every customer has unique requirements. That is why we develop tailor-made solutions that are precisely aligned with your business model, your target group and your growth goals. Whether you are an established trader looking to scale or a start-up in e-commerce aiming for a fast start – we will find the optimal partnership structure for your success.

Contact us for a personal consultation on how we can transform your specific requirements into a sustainable competitive strategy.
