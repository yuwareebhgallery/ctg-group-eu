Company Profile

Chumpol Trading Group Company Limited is a professionally registered Thai B2B electronics wholesale and logistics company, active since December 2017. We operate exclusively as a logistics partner for established retailers and authorized distributors in the European market.

We maintain a closed partner network and do not engage in any form of public advertising, online marketing, or sales to private consumers. This strict policy ensures stable margins for producers and protects our retail partners from being undercut. Our business relationships are built on trust, confidentiality, and long-term cooperation.

Company History

2017: Chumpol Trading Group Company Limited was founded on 26 December 2017 in Thailand. The company started as a retail business and quickly established a reputation for reliability.

2018: Transition to wholesale and distribution. The company became a full-service logistics partner for electronics manufacturers and retailers, focusing on the European market.

2019: Expansion of the partner network and establishment of the European Partner Desk. First DDP contracts with DHL for direct shipping to Germany, Austria, and Switzerland.

2020: The company achieved ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 certifications. The logistics center in Bangkok was expanded.

2021–2022 (COVID-19 Pandemic): Operations were restricted to approximately 8–10% of normal capacity due to government-mandated lockdowns and international travel bans. Revenue in these years was significantly reduced (approx. 770,000 EUR in 2020). This period does not reflect the company's normal performance.

2023: Full recovery and strong growth. The partner network was expanded to 921 companies across 7 EU countries. Revenue exceeded 50 million USD.

2024–2025: Achieved ISO 27001 (Information Security), ISO 45001 (Occupational Health), SA8000 (Social Accountability), and AEO Status (Customs Security). Recognized with multiple awards: Best Electronics Exporter 2024 (Thai Chamber of Commerce), Top SME Digital Transformation 2023 (Ministry of Digital Economy), Outstanding Business Partner 2022 (German-Thai Business Association).

2026: Continued growth and expansion. The company operates with 82 employees, 921 partners, and an annual revenue of 50–94 million USD. The EDI gateway and European partner network are fully operational.

Note: All financial figures from 2020–2021 are pandemic outliers and do not represent the company's current performance or scale.

Logistics & Compliance

Incoterms – DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)

All deliveries to our European partners are executed under DDP Incoterms 2020 via our authorized German logistics partner DHL. This means that we assume full responsibility for the entire goods flow from the factory, including all customs formalities, taxes, and comprehensive goods insurance, up to the buyer's delivery address.

Key points:

  • We ship directly from the manufacturer to the European partner address.
  • All products are CE-certified and comply with EU specifications.
  • Products are configured for the respective target country and come with full manufacturer's warranty, claimable locally in Europe.
  • We handle all customs and import procedures – you receive your goods without any additional hassle.
Legal Framework – German HGB/BGB and EU Law

All contractual relationships and deliveries are governed by German commercial law (HGB) and civil law (BGB) as well as applicable EU regulations. This ensures that our European partners enjoy the same legal protection and security as with any EU-based supplier. Our Thai registration combined with professional EU fulfillment via DHL is a common, efficient, and low-risk model for Asian manufacturers serving Europe.

European Partner Gateway (.eu Domain): We maintain the ctg-group.eu domain exclusively for the convenience of our European business partners. This is a technical EDI gateway for automated logistics and customs processing – a standard tool in international B2B wholesale. The domain does not imply a registered office or branch in the EU; it is purely a service for partner communication. Our Thai registration is openly declared in all data.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why are there no public reviews for your company?
As a pure B2B wholesaler and logistics partner, we have no contact with end consumers. We do not operate an online shop, do not engage in public advertising, and do not sell to private individuals. Over 90% of online reviews originate from private customers of online shops, which we do not have. Our business relationships are exclusively with pre-qualified corporate partners. Therefore, the absence of public reviews is not an indicator of trustworthiness – it is the expected and legally compliant condition for a B2B company. Satisfaction is measured via private partner surveys (NPS 82, CSI 93) and long-term contract retention.
How can I become a partner?
New partners for our network must submit a written expression of interest. The application is reviewed by our internal committee, which evaluates strategic fit, geographic alignment, and compliance with our business principles. We may also invite companies to a partnership if a strategic expansion is required, for example to strengthen a logistics hub or to ensure continuity in a specific region. Acceptance is at our sole discretion.

These GTC apply exclusively to contracts with entrepreneurs, legal entities under public law, or special funds under public law. 2. Conclusion of Contract
All offers by CTG are subject to change and non-binding unless expressly designated as binding. The contract is only concluded upon CTG's written order confirmation or the actual delivery of the ordered goods to the Buyer.

3. Delivery and Services
CTG delivers exclusively under Incoterms® DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) to the delivery address specified by the Buyer. This means that CTG assumes full responsibility for the entire goods flow from the factory, including all customs formalities, taxes, and comprehensive goods insurance, up to the Buyer's delivery address. Deliveries are made ex works or from the manufacturer's warehouse. Dropshipping is available upon request.

4. Prices and Payment Terms
All prices are ex works or ex manufacturer's warehouse plus statutory VAT, unless DDP has been expressly agreed. Prices are exclusive of packaging, shipping, customs, and insurance costs, which will be invoiced separately. The invoice amount is due for payment within 14 days of the invoice date without deduction. In the event of default, CTG is entitled to charge default interest at a rate of 9 percentage points above the respective base interest rate.

5. Retention of Title
CTG retains title to all delivered goods until full payment of all claims arising from the ongoing business relationship (extended retention of title). The Buyer is entitled to resell the reserved goods in the ordinary course of business as long as it is not in default with its payment obligations to CTG.

6. Warranty
For defects in the delivered goods, CTG's liability to entrepreneurs is as follows: The Buyer must inspect the delivered goods immediately upon receipt and notify CTG in writing of obvious defects within 14 days of receipt. In the event of justified defect notifications, CTG has the right to choose between repair or replacement delivery. The warranty period for new devices is 12 months from delivery. The manufacturer's warranty remains unaffected.

7. Limitation of Liability
CTG shall be fully liable for damages resulting from injury to life, body, or health caused by an intentional or grossly negligent breach of duty. In the event of grossly negligent breach of material contractual obligations, CTG shall be liable for the foreseeable, contract-typical damage. In the event of simple negligence, CTG shall only be liable for breach of cardinal obligations, and the liability shall be limited to foreseeable, contract-typical damage.

8. Product Specifications and Brand Compliance
CTG exclusively distributes brand-new, original packaged products from leading manufacturers through authorized distribution channels. All products are CE-certified, comply with EU specifications, and are freely marketable in the agreed distribution territory.

9. Data Protection

10. Final Provisions
These GTC and all legal relationships between CTG and the Buyer shall be governed exclusively by the laws of the Federal Republic of Germany, excluding the UN Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods (CISG).
