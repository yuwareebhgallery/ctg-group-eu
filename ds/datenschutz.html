2. General Information on Data Processing

We collect and use personal data only to the extent necessary to provide a functional website and our services.   • Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR – Legal obligation
  • Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – Legitimate interests (e.g. IT security)
4. Types of Personal Data Processed
  • Master data (name, address)
  • Contact data (telephone, email)
  • Contract and business data
  • Payment data
  • Communication data
  • Technical data (IP address, browser, OS)
5. Purposes of Data Processing
  • Processing of orders and contracts
  • Handling of enquiries
  • Provision of our services
  • Compliance with legal obligations
  • Ensuring IT security and proper operation
  • Direct marketing for own similar products (existing customer advertising) – you may object at any time
6. Disclosure of Data to Third Parties

Personal data will only be disclosed if you have expressly consented, it is necessary for the performance of the contract, there is a legal obligation, or there is a legitimate interest and no overriding interests of the data subject. Recipients may include: payment service providers, logistics and shipping service providers, IT and hosting providers, tax advisors, auditors, and authorities.

7. Cookies, Tracking & Server Log Files

Our website uses only technically necessary cookies (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). No tracking or analytics tools are used. Server log files (IP, date/time, browser) are stored for 30 days for security reasons.

8. Storage Period & Erasure

Personal data is stored only as long as necessary for the processing purposes or as long as statutory retention periods exist (e.g. 6-10 years for commercial and tax records).
