Business Model & Operational Scope

We are a pure B2B electronics wholesale and logistics partner. Our business model is exclusively B2B – we supply authorised retailers and distributors within a closed partner network. We do not operate an online shop, do not sell to private consumers, and do not engage in public advertising or marketing campaigns.

Incoterms: All deliveries are executed under DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) Incoterms 2020 via our authorised German logistics partner DHL. We assume full responsibility for the entire goods flow from the factory, including customs clearance, taxes, and comprehensive cargo insurance, up to the buyer's delivery address.

Product Warranty: We distribute exclusively brand‑new, original‑packaged products from leading manufacturers through authorised distribution channels. All products are CE‑certified, comply with EU specifications, and are configured for the respective target country. The manufacturer's warranty can be claimed locally at any authorised specialist dealer in Europe.

Legal Framework: Our delivery terms and contractual relationships are designed to be fully compliant with German commercial law (HGB) and civil law (BGB), as well as applicable EU regulations. This ensures that our European partners enjoy the same legal protection and security as with any EU‑based supplier.
