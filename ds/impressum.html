Business Model & Operational Scope

We are a pure B2B electronics wholesale and logistics partner. Our business model is exclusively B2B – we supply authorised retailers and distributors within a closed partner network. We do not operate an online shop, do not sell to private consumers, and do not engage in public advertising or marketing campaigns.

Incoterms: All deliveries are executed under DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) Incoterms 2020 via our authorised German logistics partner DHL. We assume full responsibility for the entire goods flow from the factory, including customs clearance, taxes, and comprehensive cargo insurance, up to the buyer's delivery address.

Product Warranty: We distribute exclusively brand‑new, original‑packaged products from leading manufacturers through authorised distribution channels. All products are CE‑certified, comply with EU specifications, and are configured for the respective target country. The manufacturer's warranty can be claimed locally at any authorised specialist dealer in Europe.

Legal Framework: Our delivery terms and contractual relationships are designed to be fully compliant with German commercial law (HGB) and civil law (BGB), as well as applicable EU regulations. This ensures that our European partners enjoy the same legal protection and security as with any EU‑based supplier.

Company History & Financial Development

2017: Founded on 26 December 2017 in Thailand as a retail company.

2018: Transition to wholesale and distribution, becoming a full‑service logistics partner for electronics manufacturers and retailers, focusing on the European market.

2019: Expansion of the partner network and establishment of the European Partner Desk. First DDP contracts with DHL for direct shipping to Germany, Austria, and Switzerland.

2020: Achieved ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 certifications. Logistics centre in Bangkok expanded.

2021–2022 (COVID‑19 Pandemic): Operations were restricted to approximately 8–10% of normal capacity due to government‑mandated lockdowns, closed ports, and temporary import bans in various EU countries. Revenue in these years was significantly reduced (approx. 770,000 EUR in 2020). These figures are pandemic outliers and do not reflect the company's normal performance.

2023: Full recovery and strong growth. Partner network expanded to 921 companies across 7 EU countries. Revenue exceeded 50 million USD.

2024–2025: Achieved additional certifications: ISO 27001 (Information Security), ISO 45001 (Occupational Health), SA8000 (Social Accountability), and AEO Status (Customs Security). Recognised with multiple awards: Best Electronics Exporter 2024 (Thai Chamber of Commerce), Top SME Digital Transformation 2023 (Ministry of Digital Economy), Outstanding Business Partner 2022 (German‑Thai Business Association).

2026: Continued growth. The company operates with 82 employees, 921 partners, and an annual revenue of 50–94 million USD. The EDI gateway and European partner network are fully operational.
